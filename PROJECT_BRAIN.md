The Goal: Automated bug reporting tool for Android QA testing (Banking App).

When a QA engineer encounters a crash or bug:
1. Auto-generate steps to reproduce from video recording
2. Extract and analyze logs, suggest fixes to developers
3. Auto-log defects in JIRA with all details

The Tech: Python, ADB (Android Debug Bridge), Gemini 1.5 Pro, JIRA API.

The Constraint: Handle FLAG_SECURE via "Debug Build" toggles or "Disable screen share protections."
